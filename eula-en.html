3. Interpreting results

What you can read depends on the card type, issue batch, device, and records still on the card. TrafArch matches route and stop names against reference data. Entry and exit pairings, some dates, and displayed balances may involve estimates. A missing exit record does not necessarily mean a trip is still in progress. Sample journeys in a preview are not real transactions.

For a disputed balance, fare, or journey, check with the card issuer or transit operator. TrafArch is not an official statement, proof of travel, or a refund service. Test releases may not support your card or show its full history.

4. Saving, exporting, and sharing

Core card reading, decoding, and archive management take place on your device. Even if the screen shows only the last few digits, a full JSON backup or raw communication log may contain full card numbers, balances, and travel history. When reporting a single transaction, export just the fields you need or share a screenshot with identifiers concealed. Do not post a full backup in a public group.

Before clearing app data or uninstalling, back up any records you want to keep. Deleting a local archive does not erase exported files, system backups, or copies someone else has received.

You can switch it off before accepting and still read cards normally. You can change your choice later under Settings → Privacy → Firebase Analytics.
